0. Prep prawns: clean, de-tail, dry, salt, pepper
1. Chop small: sundried tomatoes, capers, 1 clove garlic, 1 jalapeno (incl seeds)
2. Sautée in olive oil
3. When hot, add the prawns and some tomato paste
4. When prawn is almost cooked, add pasta water, cream, flour, white wine
5. Salt, pepper to taste
6. Add basil and cook briefly
7. Mix with the al dente pasta, garnish with basil and freshly grated parmesan
