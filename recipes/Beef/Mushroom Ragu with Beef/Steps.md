Directions

Prepare the mushrooms
0. Preheat the oven to 400F
1. Chop the sage and rosemary as small as you have patience for
2. Chop the mushooms to bit size ish
3. Pour the rosemary, sage, a good pinch of salt, and some freshly ground pepper into an oven dish with the mushrooms
4. Add a solid Schwups of olive oil
5. Mix them all together so the mushrooms are nicely coated
6. Put in oven for ~30min

Make the Ragu
0. Chop the carrots, onion, and celery small
1. Heat some olive oil in a pan and add the chopped veggies and the gound beef
2. Let them sautee until the meat is cooked
3. Add tomato paste and mix it in well
4. Add salt, pepper, oregano, and white wine
5. Let the alcohol boil off
6. Add cream and beef broth
6. Poor in the shrooms and their juice and spices

Serve with pasta (in which case, add a spoon of pasta water to the sauce at the end)

Or use it to make a lasagna with bechamel sauce
